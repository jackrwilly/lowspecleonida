Best GTA 6 PC settings for FPS

Full GTA 6 PC settings guide goes live on PC launch day, with tested numbers. Until then, here's what usually matters most in big open-world games.

Turn down first

  • Shadows
  • Volumetric fog / clouds
  • Ray tracing
  • Crowd / traffic density

Usually safe to keep

  • Texture quality (if you have the VRAM)
  • Anisotropic filtering
  • Model detail on medium+

Free FPS

  • Upscaling (DLSS / FSR / XeSS) on Quality mode
  • Update GPU drivers
  • Close background apps and overlays
