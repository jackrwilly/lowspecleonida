Is the RTX 4060 good for GTA 6?

The RTX 4060 is one of the most common graphics cards in budget gaming PCs and prebuilts from the last few years, so a lot of PC players will be wondering if it's enough for GTA 6. Our short answer: it should be playable at 1080p with DLSS, but its 8 GB of VRAM means you'll need to be sensible with textures.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We expect the RTX 4060 to run GTA 6 at 1080p on low to medium settings with DLSS upscaling on, aiming for 30–60 FPS depending on the scene. Ray tracing will most likely need to stay off or very low. That puts it in the same "Likely, low–medium" tier as the RTX 3060 in our Will It Run checker.

This is an estimate. Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements, and the real picture could be better or worse.

The 8 GB VRAM catch

The desktop RTX 4060 has 8 GB of GDDR6 video memory on a 128-bit memory bus. In most games it's a bit faster than an RTX 3060, but the 12 GB version of the 3060 actually has more room for textures. Big modern open-world games increasingly want more than 8 GB on high texture settings, and running out of VRAM tends to cause stutter and blurry textures rather than just a lower frame rate.

Plan on medium textures to start, and only raise them if the game stays smooth. Our RTX 3060 guide and RX 6600 guide cover the same VRAM trade-off from different angles.

DLSS and frame generation

This is where the RTX 4060 has an edge over older budget cards. It supports DLSS upscaling, and as an RTX 40-series card it also supports DLSS Frame Generation, if the PC version of GTA 6 includes it. (Multi Frame Generation is limited to RTX 50-series cards.)

Frame generation can make the game look much smoother, but it works best when your base frame rate is already decent, and it can add a little input lag. Think of it as a bonus on top of a playable setup, not a fix for a slow one. It also uses some extra VRAM, which matters on an 8 GB card.

Settings to try first

  • Resolution: 1080p
  • DLSS: Quality or Balanced
  • Textures: medium
  • Shadows and volumetrics/fog: low or medium
  • Ray tracing: off
  • Frame generation: try it once your base FPS is steady

Older PC? Check the slot

The RTX 4060 uses PCIe 4.0 x8, so it only uses eight lanes. On an older PCIe 3.0 motherboard that halves the available bandwidth, which tends to hurt most when a game runs short on VRAM. Another reason to keep textures sensible.

FAQ

Is the RTX 4060 better than the RTX 3060 for GTA 6?

It's usually a bit faster and supports DLSS Frame Generation, but the 12 GB RTX 3060 has more VRAM for textures. We'd expect them to land in a similar tier.

Should I upgrade my RTX 4060 before GTA 6 comes to PC?

Not yet. Wait for official specs. If you do upgrade later, a 16 GB card like the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB or RX 9060 XT 16GB is the meaningful step up. See our budget builds.

What about the RTX 4060 laptop GPU?

Laptop versions also have 8 GB of VRAM but run at different power limits, so performance varies a lot between laptops. Our gaming laptop guide explains what to check.
