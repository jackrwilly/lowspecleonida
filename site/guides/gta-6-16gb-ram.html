Is 16GB of RAM enough for GTA 6?

RAM is one of the cheapest upgrades you can make, so it's worth getting right. Here's our take on 8, 16 and 32 GB for GTA 6.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

16 GB should be the realistic minimum, and 32 GB is the comfortable choice. Many recent open-world games already list 16 GB as their minimum and recommend more for high settings.

8 GB

We'd expect 8 GB to struggle badly, with stuttering and long load times. If you're on 8 GB, upgrading RAM is one of the best-value moves you can make.

16 GB

Should be playable, especially if you close background apps like browsers and Discord while playing. Make sure it's running in dual channel (two sticks, not one).

32 GB

Gives you headroom for streaming, mods and background apps. If you're buying RAM now, 32 GB (2×16 GB) is the sweet spot.

Quick checks

  • See your RAM: Task Manager → Performance → Memory
  • Make sure XMP/EXPO is turned on in your BIOS so RAM runs at its rated speed
  • Match your type: DDR4 boards need DDR4, DDR5 boards need DDR5

FAQ

Does faster RAM matter?

A bit, especially on AMD Ryzen CPUs. But having enough RAM matters much more than speed.

DDR4 or DDR5?

Whatever your motherboard supports. You can't mix them.
