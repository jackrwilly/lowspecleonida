Is 8GB of VRAM enough for GTA 6?

A huge number of budget and mid-range graphics cards have 8 GB of video memory (VRAM): the RTX 4060, RTX 3060 Ti, RX 6600, RX 7600 and more. So whether 8 GB is enough is one of the most important questions for GTA 6 on PC. Our short answer: it should be playable at 1080p, but 8 GB is likely to be the thing that decides your texture setting.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We expect 8 GB of VRAM to be workable at 1080p with medium textures, plus upscaling (DLSS, FSR or XeSS). High or ultra textures, 1440p and ray tracing are where 8 GB is most likely to run out. We'd treat 12 GB as comfortable for 1080p and 16 GB as the safe choice if you're buying a card now.

This is an estimate. Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements, and the real number could be higher or lower.

What VRAM actually does

VRAM is the graphics card's own memory. It holds textures, models, shadows and other data the GPU needs right now. Open-world games like GTA keep streaming new areas in as you move, so they tend to need a lot of it. VRAM is separate from your system RAM (see Is 16GB RAM enough?), and adding more system RAM doesn't add VRAM.

What running out looks like

Running low on VRAM usually doesn't just lower your average FPS. Instead you tend to see:

  • Sudden stutters and hitches, especially when driving fast
  • Blurry textures that load in late or never sharpen
  • Performance that gets worse the longer you play

If you see these, lower texture quality first. It's the setting with the biggest effect on VRAM, and it often makes the biggest difference on an 8 GB card.

Settings that use the most VRAM

  • Texture quality: the biggest one. Start at medium.
  • Resolution: 1440p and 4K use more than 1080p. Upscaling helps by rendering at a lower resolution.
  • Ray tracing: adds extra data on top. Keep it off on 8 GB.
  • Frame generation: also uses some VRAM, so leave a little headroom.
  • Shadow quality: high settings can add up.

Our settings page covers what to turn down first.

Check how much you have

Open Task Manager → Performance → GPU and look at "Dedicated GPU memory." Some cards come in versions with different amounts, like the RTX 3060 (8 GB and 12 GB) or RTX 5060 Ti (8 GB and 16 GB), so check yours rather than going by the model name. Card-specific estimates are in our RTX 4060, RX 6600 and RTX 3060 guides.

FAQ

Should I upgrade my 8 GB card before GTA 6 comes to PC?

Not yet. Wait for official specs. If you do upgrade, a 16 GB card like the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB or RX 9060 XT 16GB is the meaningful step. See our budget builds.

Is a 12 GB card worth it over 8 GB?

For big open-world games, extra VRAM usually means you can run higher textures without stutter. Between two similar-speed cards, we'd pick the one with more VRAM.

Will upscaling fix an 8 GB limit?

It helps, because the game renders at a lower resolution, but it doesn't remove the need to keep textures sensible.
