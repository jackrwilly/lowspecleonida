Can an RX 6600 run GTA 6?

The Radeon RX 6600 has been one of the best-value budget cards for years, so plenty of PC players are wondering if it will be enough for GTA 6. Our short answer: it should be playable at 1080p on low settings with upscaling, but it will be tight.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We expect the RX 6600 to run GTA 6 at 1080p on low settings, with upscaling (FSR) turned on, likely aiming for around 30 FPS rather than a locked 60. Ray tracing should stay off. That puts it in the "Maybe, on low" tier of our Will It Run checker, alongside the RTX 2060 and RTX 3050.

This is an estimate. Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements, and the real numbers could land higher or lower.

The 8 GB VRAM question

The RX 6600 has 8 GB of GDDR6 video memory. That's enough for most games at 1080p, but large modern open-world games increasingly push past it on high texture settings. When a game runs out of VRAM you usually see stutter and blurry textures popping in, not just a lower frame rate.

Plan on medium (or lower) textures. If you want to check how much VRAM your card has, open Task Manager → Performance → GPU and look at "Dedicated GPU memory." For more on this, compare with our RTX 3060 guide: the 12 GB version of that card has more room for textures.

Upscaling: FSR, not DLSS

As an AMD card, the RX 6600 can't use NVIDIA's DLSS. It can use AMD's FSR, and Intel's XeSS also runs on non-Intel cards, if the PC version supports them. AMD has also said it is bringing FSR 4 to older Radeon cards, so check AMD's driver release notes for the current status. Upscaling will likely be the single biggest FPS boost available to RX 6600 owners.

Settings to try first

  • Resolution: 1080p
  • Upscaling: FSR on Quality or Balanced
  • Textures: medium
  • Shadows and volumetrics/fog: low
  • Ray tracing: off
  • Cap the frame rate (for example 30 or 45) for smoother, more consistent gameplay

Our settings page covers what to turn down first in more detail.

Older motherboard? One more thing

The RX 6600 connects using PCIe 4.0 x8 (eight lanes instead of the usual sixteen). On older PCIe 3.0 motherboards that halves the available bandwidth, which can hurt a little more when a game runs short on VRAM. It's not a reason to panic, but it's another reason to keep texture settings sensible.

FAQ

Should I upgrade my RX 6600 before GTA 6 comes to PC?

Not yet. Wait for official specs. If you do upgrade, a 16 GB card like the RX 9060 XT 16GB or RTX 5060 Ti 16GB is a meaningful step up. See our budget builds.

Is the RX 6600 XT much better for GTA 6?

It's faster, but it also has 8 GB of VRAM, so the same texture limits apply. Expect similar settings with a bit more FPS.

Will the RX 6600 run GTA 6 at 1440p?

We wouldn't count on it. 1080p with upscaling is the realistic target.
