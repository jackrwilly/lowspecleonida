Can an RTX 2060 run GTA 6?

The RTX 2060 was NVIDIA's entry ticket to ray tracing and DLSS back in 2019, and plenty of people are still gaming on one. With GTA 6 eventually coming to PC, it's a fair question whether it can keep up. Our short answer: maybe, at 1080p on low settings with DLSS on, but it will be tight, and its video memory is the main reason.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We put the RTX 2060 in the "Maybe, on low" tier of our Will It Run checker, alongside the RTX 3050 and RX 6600. We'd expect it to be playable at 1080p, low settings, with DLSS on Performance or Balanced, aiming for around 30 FPS-style play rather than a locked 60. Ray tracing should stay off.

This is an estimate. Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements, and the real picture could be better or worse.

6 GB of VRAM is the weak spot

The standard RTX 2060 has 6 GB of GDDR6. That was fine in 2019, but large open-world games keep streaming new areas in as you move, and that eats video memory. When you run out, you usually get stutters and blurry textures rather than just a lower frame rate. On a 6 GB card, plan on low or medium textures and keep everything else that uses VRAM modest. Our 8GB VRAM guide explains why memory matters so much, and 6 GB is a step below that.

Not every RTX 2060 is the same. NVIDIA also sold a 12 GB RTX 2060, and the RTX 2060 Super has 8 GB. Check yours in Task Manager → Performance → GPU under "Dedicated GPU memory."

DLSS is your best friend

Because it's an RTX card, the 2060 supports DLSS Super Resolution, which renders the game at a lower resolution and upscales it. That cuts GPU load and VRAM use, and it's the single biggest help for this card, assuming the PC version supports DLSS (not announced yet). What it can't use is NVIDIA's DLSS Frame Generation, which needs an RTX 40-series or newer card. If the game includes AMD's FSR frame generation, that can work on RTX 20-series cards, but frame generation feels best when you already have a decent base frame rate.

Settings to try first

  • Resolution: 1080p
  • DLSS: Balanced or Performance
  • Textures: low to medium (watch for stutter)
  • Shadows, volumetrics/fog and reflections: low
  • Ray tracing: off
  • Cap the frame rate at 30 or 40 for a steadier feel

More on what to turn down first on our settings page. Installing the game on an SSD also helps with streaming hitches (see SSD vs HDD).

Upgrade or wait?

Wait for official specs before spending money. If you do end up upgrading, the jump that matters most for GTA 6 is to a card with more VRAM, ideally 12 or 16 GB. Our budget builds page lists options, and our RTX 3060, RTX 4060 and Arc B580 guides cover the cards one tier up.

FAQ

Is the RTX 2060 better than a GTX 1660 for GTA 6?

Yes, mainly because it supports DLSS and has dedicated ray tracing hardware the GTX 1660 lacks. See our GTX 1660 guide.

Will the RTX 2060 Super do better?

Somewhat. It's faster and has 8 GB of VRAM instead of 6 GB, which gives more room for textures. Expect a similar low-to-medium experience with a bit more headroom.

Can I use ray tracing on an RTX 2060?

It technically supports it, but in a game this demanding we'd leave it off and spend the performance on a steadier frame rate.
