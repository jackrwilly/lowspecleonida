When is GTA 6 coming to PC?

GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. PC players are still waiting. Here's what's confirmed, what's rumored, and what history tells us.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

What's confirmed

Rockstar has said a PC version is planned, but it has not announced a date or even a release window. The official GTA 6 site lists only PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

What the rumors say

Some reports claim the PC version is aimed at early 2027. Others expect late 2027 or even 2028. None of these come from Rockstar, so treat them as guesses until there's an announcement.

What past Rockstar games suggest

Rockstar has always released on console first:
