Guides / Can you play GTA 6 on a gaming laptop?

Updated 2026-11-19

Can you play GTA 6 on a gaming laptop?

Yes, if it's the right laptop. Gaming laptops can run demanding games, but the same GPU name can perform very differently from one laptop to the next.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

What to look for (estimated)

  • GPU: RTX 4060 laptop or better. An RTX 5060 or 5070 laptop GPU is a safer bet.
  • RAM: 16 GB minimum, 32 GB preferred
  • Storage: 1 TB SSD (big games take a lot of space)
  • Cooling: check reviews. A hot laptop throttles and loses FPS.

The laptop GPU catch

Laptop GPUs run at different power limits (called TGP). A high-wattage RTX 4060 laptop can beat a low-wattage RTX 4070 laptop. Always check the TGP in reviews before buying.

Avoid

  • Laptops with only integrated graphics
  • Thin-and-light models with low-power GPUs
  • 8 GB of RAM that can't be upgraded

Get more FPS from a laptop

  • Always play plugged in
  • Use the laptop's performance mode
  • Use a cooling pad and keep vents clear
  • Turn on DLSS or FSR

FAQ

Is a laptop or desktop better for GTA 6?

For the same money, a desktop is usually faster and easier to upgrade. A laptop wins if you need portability.
