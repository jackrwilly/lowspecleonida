Updated 2026-11-19

Can an RTX 3060 run GTA 6?

The RTX 3060 is one of the most popular graphics cards on Steam, so this is one of the biggest questions for PC players. Short answer: probably yes, at 1080p with sensible settings.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We expect the RTX 3060 to run GTA 6 at 1080p on low to medium settings, likely with DLSS upscaling turned on, targeting 30–60 FPS. Expect to keep ray tracing off.

Why it should hold up

The 12 GB version of the RTX 3060 has more video memory (VRAM) than many newer budget cards. Big open-world games eat VRAM for textures, so that 12 GB is a real advantage. It also supports DLSS, which can add a lot of FPS.

Watch out: the 8 GB version

There's also an RTX 3060 with only 8 GB of VRAM. If that's yours, expect to drop texture quality further. Check your card in Windows: open Task Manager → Performance → GPU and look at "Dedicated GPU memory."

Settings to try first

  • DLSS on Quality or Balanced
  • Shadows: medium or low
  • Volumetrics / fog: low
  • Ray tracing: off
  • Textures: high on 12 GB, medium on 8 GB

Should you upgrade?

Not yet. Wait for official specs. If you do need more, the next real step up is a 16 GB card like the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB or RX 9060 XT 16GB. See our builds page.

FAQ

Will the RTX 3060 run GTA 6 at 1440p?

Probably only on low settings with aggressive upscaling. 1080p is the realistic target.

Is the RTX 3060 still worth buying for GTA 6?

A used 12 GB model can be good value for a budget build. New, a current 16 GB card is usually the smarter buy.
