Can the Intel Arc B580 run GTA 6?

Intel's Arc B580 became a popular budget pick thanks to its 12 GB of video memory, something rival cards in its class often lack. Our short answer: it should be a reasonable 1080p card for GTA 6 on PC, as long as your system is set up for it.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We expect the Arc B580 to run GTA 6 at 1080p on low to medium settings with upscaling on, aiming for 30–60 FPS depending on the scene, with ray tracing off or very low. Launch reviews generally found it competitive with the RTX 4060, so we'd put it in the same "Likely, low–medium" tier as the RTX 3060 and RTX 4060 in our Will It Run checker.

This is an estimate. Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements, and Intel driver support for any new game is something to check at launch.

12 GB of VRAM is the big plus

The B580 has 12 GB of GDDR6 on a 192-bit bus. That's more than the 8 GB on cards like the RTX 4060 and RX 7600, and it gives you more room for texture quality in a big open-world game before stutter sets in. See Is 8GB of VRAM enough? for why that matters.

XeSS 2: upscaling and frame generation

The B580 can't use NVIDIA's DLSS. Instead it supports Intel's XeSS 2, which includes XeSS Super Resolution (upscaling), XeSS Frame Generation and Xe Low Latency. Whether GTA 6's PC version supports XeSS hasn't been announced. AMD's FSR also works on Intel cards if the game includes it, so you should have some upscaling option either way.

Don't skip: Resizable BAR

Intel recommends turning on Resizable BAR (AMD calls it Smart Access Memory) for good performance on Arc cards, and reviewers treat it as essential. Older PCs may have it off or not support it at all. Check before buying:

  • Look in your BIOS for "Resizable BAR" or "Re-Size BAR Support" and enable it (you may also need "Above 4G Decoding" on).
  • You may need a BIOS update on older boards.
  • Intel's Arc software can show whether it's active.

The B580 also uses PCIe 4.0 x8, so on an older PCIe 3.0 board it gets half the bandwidth. If your PC is older, our Ryzen 5 3600 guide covers CPU concerns too.

Settings to try first

  • Resolution: 1080p
  • Upscaling: XeSS (or FSR) on Quality or Balanced
  • Textures: medium to high (the 12 GB helps here)
  • Shadows and volumetrics/fog: low or medium
  • Ray tracing: off
  • Keep Intel graphics drivers up to date, especially around launch

FAQ

Is the Arc B580 better than the RTX 4060 for GTA 6?

They should be in a similar tier. The B580 has more VRAM; the RTX 4060 has DLSS and NVIDIA's more established driver track record. Neither is a clear winner until we see the PC version.

Should I buy an Arc B580 for GTA 6 now?

Wait for official specs if you can. If you do buy one, make sure your PC supports Resizable BAR first. Compare it against the options on our builds page.

What about the Arc B570?

It's a cut-down version of the same chip with 10 GB of VRAM, so expect a similar setup with somewhat lower performance.
