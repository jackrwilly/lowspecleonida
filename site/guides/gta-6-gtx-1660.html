Will GTA 6 run on a GTX 1660?

The GTX 1660 family was a budget hero for years. For GTA 6, though, we think it's going to fall short.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We expect the GTX 1660, 1660 Super and 1660 Ti to be below what GTA 6 needs for a good experience. It may launch on the lowest settings, but we wouldn't count on smooth gameplay.

Why

  • Only 6 GB of VRAM. Big modern open-world games want more.
  • No DLSS support (it's not an RTX card), though AMD's FSR may still help.
  • No hardware ray tracing.

Cheapest upgrade paths

If you only upgrade your graphics card, look at a used RTX 3060 12GB or a new RX 7600 for the tightest budget, or a 16 GB card like the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB / RX 9060 XT 16GB for more headroom. Check that your power supply can handle the new card. See our build tiers.

FAQ

Should I upgrade now or wait?

Wait until official specs are out unless you find a great deal. Prices generally drop over time.

Will FSR save my 1660?

Upscaling helps, but it can't fix the VRAM limit.
