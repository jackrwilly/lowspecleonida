Will GTA 6 run on Steam Deck?

Playing GTA 6 in bed or on a train is a tempting idea, and the Steam Deck is the handheld most people already own. Our short answer: we don't expect it to run well, and even whether it runs at all may depend on things that have nothing to do with performance.

Heads up: Rockstar has not released official GTA 6 PC system requirements yet. Everything below is an estimate based on how demanding current open-world games are. We will update this page the day official specs drop.

Our estimate

We think the Steam Deck will be well below what GTA 6 needs for a good experience. Even if the game launches and runs, expect very low settings, heavy upscaling and frame rates that struggle in busy areas. We'd treat it as "maybe, for curiosity" rather than a way to actually play through the game.

This is an estimate. Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements or said anything about Steam Deck support.

Why the hardware is a stretch

The Steam Deck uses a custom AMD chip with a 4-core, 8-thread Zen 2 CPU and an RDNA 2 GPU with 8 compute units, sharing 16 GB of LPDDR5 memory between the CPU and graphics. GTA 6 was built for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, which have 8-core Zen 2 CPUs and much larger GPUs. Open-world cities lean hard on the CPU (see our Ryzen 5 3600 guide for why), and half the cores of a console is a big gap. The Deck's low 1280×800 screen helps the GPU side, but it doesn't help the CPU.

The Steam Deck OLED has a more efficient chip and faster memory, but the same core counts, so we wouldn't expect a dramatically different result.

Anti-cheat may matter more than speed

The Steam Deck runs SteamOS, which is Linux-based and plays Windows games through Valve's Proton compatibility layer. That works well for many games, but not ones whose anti-cheat blocks Linux. There's a relevant precedent: when Rockstar added BattlEye anti-cheat to GTA V in September 2024, GTA Online stopped working on Steam Deck and Linux. Story mode remained playable. We don't know yet what GTA 6's PC version will use, so check Steam Deck compatibility reports at launch before buying for it.

What about Windows handhelds?

Handhelds that run Windows, like the ROG Ally or Legion Go, avoid the Linux question and some have stronger chips than the Steam Deck. They're still far smaller than a desktop graphics card, and they share memory between CPU and GPU, so we'd expect the same story: possible on minimum settings at best. If portability matters, a gaming laptop with a dedicated GPU is a much safer bet.

A better option: stream from your PC

If you have a capable desktop, Steam Remote Play lets the PC do the work while the Steam Deck just displays the stream over your home network. Results depend on your Wi-Fi, but it sidesteps the hardware limits entirely. Not sure your desktop is up to it? Try our Will It Run checker or see our budget builds.

FAQ

Will Rockstar make GTA 6 Steam Deck Verified?

Nothing has been announced. Valve's "Verified" ratings come from Valve's testing after a game is on Steam, so we'll know after the PC release.

Can I install Windows on my Steam Deck for GTA 6?

You can install Windows on a Steam Deck, which can get around Linux anti-cheat blocks, but it won't make the hardware any faster.

Is the Steam Deck OLED better for GTA 6?

A little, thanks to faster memory, but it has the same 4-core CPU and 8-CU GPU, so don't expect a big difference.
